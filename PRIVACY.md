# Clean Volume Booster privacy policy

Updated November 13, 2026.

Clean Volume Booster is maintained at https://github.com/shrkawy/volume-booster.

## Data used by the extension

When you choose to boost a tab, the extension captures that tab's audio and processes it inside your browser using the Web Audio API. It does not record or upload audio.

When you open the popup, the extension checks the current tab for audio or video elements and reads tab information to show the tab's title and icon. It does not save a browsing history.

Your last boost level and speaker protection preference are saved in Chrome's local extension storage. Active tab settings are held in session storage. These settings are not sent to the developer.

## Sharing and services

The extension has no analytics, advertising SDKs, account system, or developer-operated server. It does not sell or transmit user data to the developer or third parties. Chrome and the Chrome Web Store may process information independently under Google's own policies.

## Control and contact

Stop boosting to end audio capture. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage. For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/shrkawy/volume-booster/issues. GitHub issues are public, so do not include private information.
